Privacy

Privacy policy

AccessScan is a free, hosted service with nothing to track: no accounts, no cookies, no analytics. This page states exactly what data exists and where it goes.

What data exists

Only two things: the URLs you submit and the scan results for them. On our side, both are held in the memory (RAM) of the hosted server process. There is no database, no scan log file, and nothing is written to disk. Scan records are capped at the 20 most recent and are deleted when the server process restarts.

On your side, the browser keeps a list of your recent scans (scan IDs and URLs) in localStorage so the "Recent scans" list survives page reloads. This data stays on your device only and is never transmitted to us or anyone else. No cookies are set and no other browser storage is used.

What is sent where

AccessScan is hosted: the frontend is served from Vercel and the scanning backend runs on Render. Your browser communicates only with AccessScan; the single outbound action performed on your behalf is the purpose of the tool itself: the server retrieves the website URL you supplied and performs an accessibility check on the rendered page, in the same manner as your browser. The target site receives a normal page request bearing a User-Agent string that identifies AccessScan.

There are no third-party requests: no analytics, no telemetry, no tracking pixels, no external fonts or trackers. Every script and stylesheet is served by the same hosted deployment. The only external links on this site are documentation references (such as the axe-core rule index) that load only if you choose to open them.

What AccessScan does not do

  • Set cookies or track users in any form
  • Require accounts, registration, or login
  • Collect analytics or usage statistics
  • Load third-party SDKs or external services
  • Process payments; the tool is free
  • Persist scan data beyond in-memory storage

Data retention

Scan records are retained in server memory only, capped at the 20 most recent scans, and are deleted when the server process stops or restarts. Your browser’s localStorage keeps up to 20 recent scan IDs and URLs until you clear site data or the list ages out. To keep a copy of a report, export it (HTML, JSON, or PDF) before the server restarts. Exports are generated on demand and downloaded to your browser like any ordinary file.

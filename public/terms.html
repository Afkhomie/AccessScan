What a scan is — and isn't

Automated scans find only a fraction of real accessibility issues — research puts it at roughly 30–57% of WCAG problems (per Deque). A clean report does not mean a site is compliant with WCAG or the European Accessibility Act, and flagged issues are a starting point for fixes, not a legal verdict. Always verify with a manual audit when compliance matters. EAA enforcement applies in the EU since June 2025 — this tool is a helper, not a substitute for expertise.

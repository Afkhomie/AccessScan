What a scan is and is not

Automated scans detect only a fraction of real accessibility issues. Published research places the figure at approximately 30–57% of WCAG problems (per Deque). A clean report does not mean that a site complies with WCAG or the European Accessibility Act, and flagged issues are a starting point for remediation, not a legal determination. Where compliance matters, verify results with a manual audit. EAA enforcement applies in the EU as of June 2025. This tool is an aid, not a substitute for professional expertise.
